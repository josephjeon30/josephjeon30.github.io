NKI to SFZ

Turn Kontakt instruments and their samples into SFZ you can load in sfizz, sforzando and other free samplers. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Pick the folder that holds the library's Instruments and Samples folders.

Good to know

      
    
        
  • Works with Kontakt 5–7 instruments whose samples are separate files (.ncw, .wav, …). Samples are decoded to WAV.
    • 
        
  • Encrypted Kontakt Player libraries, single-file “monolith” instruments, and pre-Kontakt 5 files aren't supported.
    • 
        
  • Mappings, velocity layers, tuning, volume, loops, crossfades, envelopes and release groups carry over. KSP script behaviour can't, since SFZ has no scripting.
    • 
        
  • Only convert libraries whose license lets you.
    • 
        
  • Prefer the command line? Download nki2sfz.py (Python 3 + numpy; can also write FLAC).
